- GIBC V2 offers demanding technical tracks and strong portfolio deliverables, but organizer depth, prize details, judge details, and deadline timezone remain incomplete.
- GatewayHacks is useful for beginner social-impact practice, but allows no-code or design-only submissions and has limited resume recognition.
- LaunchHacks V receives B for its fifth iteration, scale, sponsors, and industry judges, while retaining medium confidence because public age eligibility and prize composition are inconsistent.
